# Know Your Customer Standard (v2)

Synthetic standard for hackathon use only.

## §2.1 Identification and Verification
All customers must be identified and verified at onboarding. Business customers require verification of legal existence, nature of business, expected activity and beneficial owners holding 25 percent or more.

## §3.3 Expected Activity Profile
An expected activity profile, including expected countries, transaction types, volumes and values, must be captured at onboarding and used by transaction monitoring. Products that enable new activity types for existing customers must trigger a profile refresh.

## §4.1 Enhanced Due Diligence
Enhanced due diligence applies to higher-risk customers, including SMB customers with cross-border payment activity to higher-risk jurisdictions, and must be completed before the higher-risk activity is enabled.

## §6.2 Existing Customer Base
Enabling a new feature for the existing customer base without a new customer segment or geography does not require re-verification, provided the expected activity profile is updated where transaction behaviour is expected to change.
