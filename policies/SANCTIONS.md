# Sanctions Compliance Policy (v2)

Synthetic policy for hackathon use only.

## §2.1 Screening Obligation
All customers, counterparties and payment messages must be screened against applicable sanctions lists before onboarding and before a payment is released. Screening must cover names, aliases and, for cross-border payments, beneficiary bank and country.

## §4.3 Third-Party Processors
Where a third-party processor performs any part of payment execution, the Bank remains responsible for sanctions compliance. The processor's screening controls must be independently tested by the Bank before launch. A vendor's self-assessment or questionnaire response is not evidence of control effectiveness.

## §5.2 Higher-Risk Destinations
Payments to or from higher-risk jurisdictions require enhanced screening thresholds and manual review of potential matches before release. Real-time or instant products must hold the payment until screening completes.

## §7.1 Control Testing
Sanctions controls must be tested before launch and at least annually. Test evidence must be retained and referenced in the product risk assessment.
