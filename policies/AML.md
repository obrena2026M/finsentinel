# Anti-Money Laundering Policy (v3)

Synthetic policy for hackathon use only. Not a real institution's policy.

## §1.1 Purpose
This Policy sets the minimum anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing requirements for all products, channels and customer segments. New products and material changes must be assessed for financial crime risk before launch.

## §2.1 Risk-Based Approach
The Bank applies a risk-based approach. Inherent risk is assessed across product, customer, geography, channel, transaction, third party, technology and process dimensions. Controls may reduce residual risk but do not eliminate inherent risk.

## §3.2 Customer Segments
Small and medium business (SMB) customers present elevated risk where beneficial ownership is opaque or where the business handles cash-intensive or cross-border activity. Enhanced due diligence applies to SMB customers with cross-border payment activity.

## §4.1 New Product Approval
No new product, feature, channel, geography or third-party arrangement may launch without a completed financial crime risk assessment reviewed by an FCRM analyst and approved by the Risk Committee. Approval may be conditional.

## §5.3 Higher-Risk Geographies
Cross-border activity involving jurisdictions on the Bank's higher-risk country list requires enhanced due diligence and enhanced transaction monitoring. Mexico and Brazil are designated higher-risk jurisdictions for cross-border payment products. Canada is designated standard risk.

## §5.4 Cross-Border Payments
Cross-border payment products must implement sender and beneficiary screening, originator information capture and destination-country risk scoring before launch. Instant settlement increases exposure because funds cannot be recalled once settled.

## §6.1 Non-Face-to-Face Channels
API, mobile and other non-face-to-face channels require device or credential authentication, identity verification at onboarding and behavioural monitoring proportional to transaction value.

## §7.2 Record Keeping and Audit
All risk assessments, overrides and approval decisions must be recorded in an immutable audit trail retaining the original AI or analyst output, the changed value, the rationale, the user and the timestamp.
