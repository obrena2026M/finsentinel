# Third-Party Risk Management Standard (v1)

Synthetic standard for hackathon use only.

## §2.2 Due Diligence Before Engagement
Third parties that process payments, hold customer data or perform compliance functions are critical vendors. Critical vendors require financial crime due diligence, including review of AML program documentation, regulatory history and independent audit reports, before contract signature.

## §3.1 Vendor Assertions
Statements made by a vendor about its own controls must be verified through documentation, testing or independent assurance. Unverified vendor assertions must be treated as providing no mitigation in the risk assessment until verified.

## §4.4 Ongoing Monitoring
Critical vendors must be reviewed at least annually and on any material change in service scope, geography or ownership. Payment processors operating in new jurisdictions trigger a re-assessment.

## §5.1 Fourth Parties
Where a vendor subcontracts payment execution or screening to another party, the fourth party must be identified and assessed to the same standard.
