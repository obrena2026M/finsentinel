# Geographic Expansion Proposal: Business Accounts in Mexico

## 1. Summary
We propose to offer business operating accounts to Mexican-incorporated SMBs that have a Canadian parent company. Accounts will support inbound and outbound MXN wires and CAD conversion.

## 2. Customer Segment
SMB customers incorporated in Mexico with an existing Canadian parent relationship. Approximately 400 customers are expected in the first year.

## 3. Geographies
Mexico is the new operating geography. Funds will move between Mexico and Canada. Beneficial owners may reside in Mexico, Canada or the United States.

## 4. Channel and Technology
Onboarding and servicing through the existing relationship-manager channel. No third party is involved for account servicing; correspondent banking uses the existing Mexican correspondent.

## 5. Transaction Characteristics
Expected transaction volume is 6,000 wires per month. Average transaction value is CAD 22,000 with a maximum of CAD 500,000. Transaction types are inbound and outbound wire transfers.

## 6. Controls
Sanctions screening applies to all wires. Monitoring coverage: existing cross-border wire scenarios apply; no Mexico-specific scenario exists yet. Enhanced due diligence will be applied at onboarding.

## 7. Process
Spanish-language documentation review will be performed by two bilingual relationship managers. No other process change.
