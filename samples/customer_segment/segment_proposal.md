# Customer Segment Proposal: Money Service Businesses

## 1. Summary
We propose to open the commercial operating account to licensed money service businesses, specifically currency exchange and remittance agents registered in Canada. This segment is currently excluded by policy.

## 2. Customer Segment
Money service businesses registered with the national regulator. Expected 120 customers in year one. Enhanced due diligence will be mandatory.

## 3. Geographies
Canada only for account domicile. Customers remit funds globally, including to higher-risk jurisdictions.

## 4. Channel and Technology
Existing branch and relationship-manager channel. No third party is involved. No technology change.

## 5. Transaction Characteristics
Expected transaction volume is 40,000 transactions per month across the segment. Average transaction value is CAD 2,500 with high velocity of cash deposits and outbound wires. Transaction types include cash deposits, wires and e-transfers.

## 6. Controls
Sanctions screening applies to all wires. Monitoring coverage: a dedicated MSB monitoring scenario set is planned; existing scenarios do not cover cash-intensive remittance activity. Annual EDD reviews and agent-level activity reporting are proposed.

## 7. Process
A specialist onboarding team will be trained on MSB licensing verification. Ongoing agent reviews will be a new quarterly process.
