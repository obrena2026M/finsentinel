# Channel Proposal: WhatsApp Banking

## 1. Summary
We propose to enable balance enquiries, payee management and domestic transfers up to CAD 1,000 through a WhatsApp Business conversational channel. The channel is operated with a messaging vendor and is available to existing verified retail customers.

## 2. Customer Segment
Existing retail customers who have completed KYC and enrolled in mobile banking. No new customer segment.

## 3. Geographies
Canada only. Domestic transfers to Canadian payees.

## 4. Channel and Technology
WhatsApp is a new non-face-to-face channel. Messages are routed through the third-party messaging vendor ChatBridge Inc. Authentication uses a one-time passcode sent to the enrolled mobile device plus a conversational PIN.

## 5. Transaction Characteristics
Expected transaction volume is 25,000 transfers per month. Maximum transaction value is CAD 1,000 with a daily limit of CAD 2,000. Transaction type is domestic e-transfer.

## 6. Controls
Sanctions screening is not applicable to domestic e-transfers between Canadian accounts. Monitoring coverage: existing e-transfer scenarios apply; channel-specific device and session monitoring is not yet in place. Payee additions require a 24-hour cooling period.

## 7. Process
Customer support will receive a new escalation path for conversational channel disputes. No other process change.
