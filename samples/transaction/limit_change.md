# Transaction Change Proposal: Daily Transfer Limit Increase

## 1. Summary
We propose to increase the retail e-transfer daily limit from CAD 3,000 to CAD 10,000 and the per-transaction limit from CAD 3,000 to CAD 5,000 for customers with at least 12 months tenure and no fraud flags.

## 2. Customer Segment
Existing retail customers meeting the tenure and fraud-flag criteria. Approximately 60 percent of the retail base qualifies. No new customer segment.

## 3. Geographies
Canada only. Domestic e-transfers.

## 4. Channel and Technology
Existing mobile and web channels. No third party is involved. A limit-eligibility rule will be added to the existing payments engine.

## 5. Transaction Characteristics
Expected transaction volume is unchanged at 1.2 million e-transfers per month, with average value expected to rise from CAD 410 to CAD 620. Maximum transaction value becomes CAD 5,000. Transaction type is domestic e-transfer.

## 6. Controls
Sanctions screening is not applicable to domestic e-transfers. Monitoring coverage: existing e-transfer scenarios are tuned to the current CAD 3,000 limit and their thresholds will need re-tuning. Velocity limits remain at 10 transfers per day.

## 7. Process
No operational process change. Fraud operations will monitor the first 90 days with a weekly report.
