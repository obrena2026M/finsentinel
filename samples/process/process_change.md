# Process Change Proposal: Manual Wire Exception Handling

## 1. Summary
Failed outbound wire payments currently route to an automated repair queue. While the repair engine is rebuilt, exceptions will be handled manually by the wire operations team for a period of six months. Both domestic and cross-border wires are affected.

## 2. Customer Segment
No change to customer segment. All existing retail and commercial customers who send wires are affected.

## 3. Geographies
Canada for domestic wires. Cross-border wires to existing corridors including the United States, the United Kingdom and Mexico.

## 4. Channel and Technology
No customer channel change. Operations staff will use the existing payment operations workstation. No third party is involved. A shared spreadsheet will be used to track exceptions until the repair engine returns.

## 5. Transaction Characteristics
Approximately 900 wire exceptions per month. Average exception value is CAD 18,000 with a maximum of CAD 250,000. Transaction type is outbound wire transfer.

## 6. Controls
Sanctions screening has already completed before a wire reaches the exception queue. Monitoring coverage: repaired wires re-enter the standard wire monitoring scenarios. Four-eyes approval applies to manual repairs above CAD 50,000.

## 7. Process
Manual repair introduces a new operational step: operators may edit beneficiary details before release. Edits below CAD 50,000 are single-operator. Exception ageing will be reported weekly.
